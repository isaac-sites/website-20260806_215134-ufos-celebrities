## Introduction

The most striking feature shared by May Pang's and [John Lennon]({{ 'john-lennon/' | relative_url }})'s accounts of their reported UFO sighting on the evening of 23 August 1974 is not simply that they both claimed to have witnessed an unidentified object over Manhattan. Rather, both consistently described an unusual and recognisable pattern of lights: a single steady red light positioned above a dark central object and a ring of flashing white lights arranged around its outer rim. Alongside those lighting details, both witnesses described the object as moving slowly and silently at what appeared to be relatively low altitude.

These matching visual elements have become one of the strongest internal consistencies within the historical record of the sighting. They do not establish that the object was extraterrestrial or even that it was an unconventional aircraft. However, because the agreement extends beyond a vague recollection of "strange lights" into multiple specific visual characteristics, researchers and writers discussing the incident frequently treat the matching descriptions as the most evidentially interesting aspect of May Pang's role as a second witness. The value of these similarities lies in their specificity rather than in any proof of the object's identity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.good.is/john-lennon-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: good.is">[Good.is]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">good.is</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon had a UFO sighting so vivid, he referenced the phenomenon in a song</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John Lennon had a UFO sighting so vivid, he referenced the phenomenon in a song</span></span></span>

May Pang's later descriptions repeatedly refer to a large circular or flattened-cone-shaped object carrying a brilliant red light positioned on top. She distinguished this light from those of ordinary aircraft by noting that it remained steady rather than pulsating. As the object approached, she said she could also see an entire circle of bright white lights running around the rim of the craft, flashing on and off in sequence. In later interviews she emphasised that these lights were numerous enough to appear dazzling rather than consisting of only one or two navigation lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geocities.ws/MayPangStory/UFO.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geocities.ws">[Geocities]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geocities.ws</span><span class="citation-popover-title">May Pang UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May Pang UFO...</span></span></span>

## Agreement on Shape, Movement and Silence

The matching light pattern gains additional significance because it appears alongside agreement on several other observational details.

Both Pang and Lennon described the object as fundamentally dark in colour, with the lights appearing attached to or surrounding the structure rather than constituting the object itself. Neither recalled a luminous sphere or an indistinct glow. Instead, the lights outlined what they believed was a substantial physical object.

Their descriptions of movement also align closely. Pang recalled the craft moving slowly enough that the couple could observe it continuously as it crossed nearby rooftops before travelling along the East River. Lennon likewise described watching it for several minutes as it moved deliberately rather than rapidly across the skyline. Neither account suggests erratic darting or instantaneous acceleration during the portion they principally described.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.beatlesstory.com/blog/john-lennon-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beatlesstory.com">[The Beatles Story Museum, Liverpool]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beatlesstory.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Beatles Story Museum, Liverpool...</span></span></span>

Silence forms another major point of agreement.

Pang repeatedly remarked that the object passed with no audible engine noise despite appearing only a few storeys above neighbouring buildings. Lennon independently stressed exactly the same point, explaining that he could clearly hear ordinary city sounds—including traffic below—yet heard nothing from the object itself. That comparison formed part of his own reasoning for rejecting a helicopter as the obvious explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.good.is/john-lennon-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: good.is">[Good.is]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">good.is</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon had a UFO sighting so vivid, he referenced the phenomenon in a song</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John Lennon had a UFO sighting so vivid, he referenced the phenomenon in a song</span></span></span>

## The Importance of the Specific Light Pattern

From an evidential perspective, the lighting arrangement matters because it is comparatively unusual.

Witnesses frequently report unidentified lights in the sky, but many such reports involve only isolated points of light with few distinguishing characteristics. Pang and Lennon instead described a structured configuration consisting of multiple white flashing lights arranged around the object's circumference together with one steady red light above.

That degree of detail gives later investigators something concrete to compare across independent recollections. Researchers interested in witness consistency often distinguish between broad agreement ("we saw something strange") and agreement regarding multiple independent observational features. The latter generally carries greater weight because it is statistically less likely to emerge from vague memory alone.

The shared lighting configuration therefore represents one of the strongest internal consistencies within the case. It does not identify the object, but it provides a richer basis for comparing the witnesses' recollections than would a simple report of an unidentified light.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geocities.ws/MayPangStory/UFO.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geocities.ws">[Geocities]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geocities.ws</span><span class="citation-popover-title">May Pang UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May Pang UFO...</span></span></span>

## How Consistent Were Their Descriptions Over Time?

Although Pang and Lennon described the event on different occasions and in different contexts, the central lighting pattern remained notably stable.

Pang elaborated her observations in interviews and later writings, providing additional information about the apparent brightness of the white lights and the non-pulsating character of the red light. Lennon generally offered shorter descriptions, yet he repeatedly returned to the same essential arrangement: a dark object, flashing white lights around the lower edge and one red light on top.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geocities.ws/MayPangStory/UFO.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geocities.ws">[Geocities]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geocities.ws</span><span class="citation-popover-title">May Pang UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May Pang UFO...</span></span></span>

Minor differences certainly exist.

Some versions describe the object as circular, while others compare it to a flattened cone or saucer. Estimates of altitude and distance vary somewhat, and later retellings differ over exactly how the object departed. These are the kinds of variations commonly found in eyewitness narratives recorded years after an event.

Notably, however, those variations do not substantially affect the central description of the lighting pattern. Across decades of retelling, the arrangement of the red and white lights remained one of the least variable parts of the narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.beatlesstory.com/blog/john-lennon-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beatlesstory.com">[The Beatles Story Museum, Liverpool]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beatlesstory.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Beatles Story Museum, Liverpool...</span></span></span>

## Why the Matching Details Are Significant—but Not Conclusive

The matching descriptions strengthen the historical credibility of one limited proposition: that Pang and Lennon both believed they had observed the same unusual aerial object.

They do not establish what that object actually was.

Several reasons prevent stronger conclusions.

First, the two witnesses observed the object together from the same balcony. They were therefore not independent observers separated by time or location. They could immediately discuss what they were seeing, and later recollections may have influenced one another.

Second, no surviving photograph confirms the reported lighting arrangement. Lennon reportedly attempted photographs using both a Polaroid camera and a conventional camera, but no useful images resulted. Without contemporaneous visual evidence, later descriptions necessarily rely on memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.co.uk/this-day-in-history/23-august/john-lennon-sees-a-ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.co.uk">[Sky HISTORY TV channel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sky HISTORY TV channel John Lennon sees a UFO &#124; Sky HISTORY TV Channel</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sky HISTORY TV channel John Lennon sees a UFO &#124; Sky HISTORY TV Channel</span></span></span>

Third, although Lennon and Pang reportedly contacted police after the sighting and were told that other people had reported unusual lights that evening, no detailed official investigative record preserving those additional witness descriptions has become part of the public historical record. ## Why These Matches Continue to Receive Attention

Among the many celebrity UFO stories, the Lennon–Pang incident stands out because of the specificity of the shared observations.

Many celebrity accounts rely on a single narrator recounting an experience decades later. In contrast, this case includes two witnesses who consistently described an object displaying the same unusual arrangement of lights. The agreement encompasses not only colour but also placement, flashing behaviour, overall shape, movement and silence.

That does not transform the sighting into proof of an extraordinary phenomenon. Alternative explanations—including misidentification of an unconventional aircraft, atmospheric effects, or another unidentified but conventional object—cannot be ruled out solely through witness testimony. Nevertheless, when assessing the internal consistency of the historical record, the matching light pattern remains one of the strongest features supporting the claim that Pang and Lennon experienced the same visual event.

For that reason, discussions of May Pang's importance as a second witness typically focus less on the broader question of whether a UFO was present and more on the unusually detailed overlap between her account and Lennon's. The recurring description of a dark object carrying a steady red upper light and flashing white rim lights is the most distinctive point where their recollections converge, making it the principal evidential contribution of Pang's testimony within this specific case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.good.is/john-lennon-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: good.is">[good.is]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">good.is</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon had a UFO sighting so vivid, he referenced the phenomenon in a song</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John Lennon had a UFO sighting so vivid, he referenced the phenomenon in a song</span></span></span>
