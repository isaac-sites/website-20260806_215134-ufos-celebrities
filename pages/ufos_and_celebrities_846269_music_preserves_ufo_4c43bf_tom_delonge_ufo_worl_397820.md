## How UFO themes became part of DeLonge's musical identity

Unlike musicians who referenced UFOs occasionally, DeLonge made the subject a recurring creative motif from the early stages of his career. Friends and bandmates have long described his fascination with extraterrestrials as genuine rather than a marketing invention, and that enthusiasm naturally entered Blink-182's songwriting. Interviews from the late 1990s and early 2000s consistently portray him as someone who read extensively about UFOs and frequently discussed the subject while touring.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nme.com/features/music-interviews/tom-delonge-played-blink-182-aliens-get-really-offended-start-inter-planetary-war-2536547" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nme.com">[nme.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nme.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tom DeLonge: “If I played Blink-182 to aliens they would get really offended and start an inter-planetary war”August 10, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 10, 2019</span></span></span>

Although written with the band's characteristic humour, the song differs from a simple novelty track. As DeLonge's public interest deepened, listeners increasingly interpreted "Aliens Exist" not merely as comic science fiction but as an early expression of beliefs he would later discuss seriously in interviews.

## Angels & Airwaves transformed UFO ideas into mythology

The formation of Angels & Airwaves in 2005 marked a significant shift in DeLonge's approach. Rather than treating extraterrestrials as an isolated lyrical topic, he developed an expansive style built around space imagery, transcendence, hidden knowledge and humanity's future.

Many Angels & Airwaves songs avoid explicit references to UFO sightings while drawing heavily on cosmic symbolism. Albums such as *We Don't Need to Whisper*, *I-Empire*, *LOVE* and later releases repeatedly employ imagery of stars, distant worlds, exploration, awakening and human evolution. This allowed DeLonge to create an atmosphere in which UFO mythology became part of a broader philosophical worldview rather than simply a collection of unexplained sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.musicradar.com/news/tom-delonge-interview-blink-182" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: musicradar.com">[musicradar.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">musicradar.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Classic Interview: Tom DeLonge talks Blink-182 evolution – &quot;We were nowhere near as good as Travis, but we got better&quot; &#124; MusicR...</span></span></span>

The result is notable because the mythology is conveyed through mood and narrative as much as through direct statements. Expansive synthesiser textures, cinematic production and recurring themes of discovery reinforce ideas that listeners already familiar with DeLonge's interests often connect to his fascination with unexplained aerial phenomena.

## From songs to a connected fictional universe

DeLonge eventually extended this approach beyond conventional albums by creating interconnected entertainment projects. The most significant is the **Sekret Machines** series, developed through novels, non-fiction books, comics, films and music under the umbrella of his entertainment company.

Rather than presenting songs as isolated artistic works, DeLonge described them as pieces of a much larger narrative exploring alleged government secrecy, advanced technology and unexplained phenomena. In interviews surrounding the launch of *Sekret Machines*, he explained that music was intended to work alongside books and visual media, creating a transmedia experience rather than a traditional concept album.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gq.com/story/tom-delonge-ufos-interview-sekret-machines-novel" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gq.com">[GQ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gq.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">tom delonge ufos interview sekret machines novel</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tom DeLonge Has Something Very Important to Tell You &#124; GQApril 2, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2016</span></span></span>

This represents an unusual development within UFO-themed music. Earlier artists often referenced individual encounters or mysteries, whereas DeLonge sought to construct an entire fictional mythology inspired by subjects he also discussed as matters deserving public investigation.

## The role of ambiguity in his songwriting

A defining characteristic of DeLonge's music is its deliberate ambiguity. Most Angels & Airwaves songs avoid presenting UFO claims as factual declarations. Instead they explore recurring questions:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Whether hidden truths exist beyond accepted knowledge.
* How individuals respond to profound discoveries.
* The relationship between fear and curiosity.
* Humanity's place within a much larger universe.

</div>

This ambiguity gives the music longevity. Songs remain meaningful for listeners who interpret them spiritually, philosophically or scientifically, even if they have no interest in UFO research.

At the same time, fans aware of DeLonge's interviews often read additional meaning into the lyrics, creating a layered listening experience in which personal beliefs, public statements and fictional storytelling reinforce one another.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nme.com/features/music-interviews/tom-delonge-angels-and-airwaves-new-music-ufo-videos-blink-182-2505687" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nme.com">[NME]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nme.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tom DeLonge on the return of Angels &amp; Airwaves, his declassified UFO videos and Blink-182...</span></span></span>

## Music and public advocacy increasingly overlapped

As DeLonge became publicly associated with UFO research through To The Stars and later television and publishing projects, the relationship between his music and his public persona changed.

His artistic output increasingly served as an entry point into wider discussions about unidentified aerial phenomena. Journalists frequently noted that the same musician who once sang "Aliens Exist" was now producing [documentaries]({{ 'documentaries/' | relative_url }}), publishing books and arguing that governments should take unidentified aerial phenomena more seriously.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nme.com/features/music-interviews/tom-delonge-angels-and-airwaves-new-music-ufo-videos-blink-182-2505687" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nme.com">[nme.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nme.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tom DeLonge on the return of Angels &amp; Airwaves, his declassified UFO videos and Blink-182...</span></span></span>

This crossover altered how earlier songs were perceived. Tracks originally heard as playful pop-punk or speculative science fiction came to be viewed retrospectively as part of a long-running personal interest that eventually shaped DeLonge's career beyond music.

## Cultural influence and continuing debate

DeLonge's musical treatment of UFO mythology has had an influence that extends beyond album sales. "Aliens Exist" remains closely associated with him in live performance and fan culture, to the extent that Blink-182 has continued performing the song even during periods when DeLonge was absent from the band. Its reputation has grown alongside renewed public attention to unidentified aerial phenomena in the United States.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nme.com/features/music-interviews/tom-delonge-played-blink-182-aliens-get-really-offended-start-inter-planetary-war-2536547" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nme.com">[NME]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nme.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tom DeLonge: “If I played Blink-182 to aliens they would get really offended and start an inter-planetary war”August 10, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 10, 2019</span></span></span>

His work has also generated continuing debate. Supporters argue that his music encouraged curiosity about unexplained phenomena long before UFO discussions entered mainstream political and media coverage. Critics respond that artistic storytelling should not be confused with evidence, noting that compelling narratives can exist independently of verifiable facts. Both perspectives recognise that his songs function primarily as cultural artefacts rather than documentation of extraordinary claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://guitar.com/features/interviews/tom-delonge-angels-airwaves-lifeforms-blink-182-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: guitar.com">[guitar.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">guitar.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on guitar.com.</span></span></span>

## Why DeLonge's music stands apart

Within the broader history of music that preserves UFO stories, DeLonge's catalogue is distinctive because it operates on several levels simultaneously.

His songs preserve themes drawn from UFO folklore, but they also connect those themes to an evolving fictional universe, recurring philosophical questions and a public campaign encouraging greater attention to unidentified aerial phenomena. Few musicians have attempted to integrate popular music, novels, documentaries and claimed investigative interests so extensively around the same central mythology.

Whether listeners approach his work as speculative fiction, symbolic art or a reflection of sincerely held beliefs, DeLonge's music has become one of the most sustained examples of UFO mythology being developed and preserved through modern popular culture rather than through isolated songs or single celebrity anecdotes.

    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
