## Introduction

Among the surviving records connected with [John Lennon]({{ 'john-lennon/' | relative_url }})'s reported UFO sighting in New York on 23 August 1974, no piece of evidence is more important than the handwritten note printed in the artwork of *Walls and Bridges*. It is significant not because it proves what Lennon saw, but because it documents, in a commercially published artefact issued only weeks after the alleged event, that Lennon himself was already claiming to have seen a UFO at a specific date and time. That makes the album packaging the strongest near-contemporary documentary evidence for the existence of the claim itself, distinguishing it from later memoirs, interviews and retellings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.johnlennon.com/music/albums/walls-and-bridges/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: johnlennon.com">[John Lennon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">johnlennon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon Walls And Bridges</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John LennonWalls And Bridges - John Lennon with The Plastic Ono Nuclear Band/Little Big Horns/and the Philharmanic OrchestrangeSeptember...</span></span></span>

> "On the 23rd Aug. 1974 at 9 o'clock I saw a U.F.O.

The statement is remarkably brief. Lennon did not describe the object's appearance, speculate about its origin or attempt to persuade readers. Instead, he recorded only three essential pieces of information:

* the date;
* the approximate time;
* the simple assertion that he saw "a U.F.O."

Its understated wording is one reason historians regard it as unusually valuable. Rather than appearing in a sensational interview or promotional campaign devoted to paranormal subjects, the statement was tucked into the artwork of a mainstream commercial album that otherwise focused on music, lyrics and personal artwork.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.johnlennon.com/music/albums/walls-and-bridges/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: johnlennon.com">[John Lennon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">johnlennon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon Walls And Bridges</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John LennonWalls And Bridges - John Lennon with The Plastic Ono Nuclear Band/Little Big Horns/and the Philharmanic OrchestrangeSeptember...</span></span></span>

## Why the Timing Makes the Note So Valuable

The principal evidential strength of the *Walls and Bridges* note lies in its proximity to the reported event.

According to the inscription, the sighting occurred on 23 August 1974 at about 9 pm. The album reached the public roughly five to six weeks later, an unusually short interval compared with many well-known UFO stories that became widely documented only years or decades afterwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.johnlennon.com/music/albums/walls-and-bridges/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: johnlennon.com">[John Lennon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">johnlennon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon Walls And Bridges</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John LennonWalls And Bridges - John Lennon with The Plastic Ono Nuclear Band/Little Big Horns/and the Philharmanic OrchestrangeSeptember...</span></span></span>

This short gap matters because it greatly reduces several common historical problems:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Memory drift.** Human recollections naturally change over time. A statement written weeks after an event is generally less vulnerable to reconstructed memory than one first recorded decades later.
* **Legend formation.** Celebrity stories often accumulate dramatic details through repeated retelling. The album note predates most later narratives.
* **Retrospective reinterpretation.** Because the statement was fixed in print in 1974, later interviews cannot reasonably be dismissed as inventions created long after the fact.

</div>

The note therefore serves as a chronological anchor. Whatever Lennon later said about the experience, researchers know that by September 1974 he was already publicly asserting that he had seen what he identified as a UFO on the specified evening.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.johnlennon.com/music/albums/walls-and-bridges/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: johnlennon.com">[John Lennon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">johnlennon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon Walls And Bridges</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John LennonWalls And Bridges - John Lennon with The Plastic Ono Nuclear Band/Little Big Horns/and the Philharmanic OrchestrangeSeptember...</span></span></span>

## What the Inscription Establishes

The album note supports several conclusions with relatively high confidence.

First, it demonstrates that Lennon genuinely wanted the claim placed into the public record while the event was still recent. Publishing the statement on a major album carried reputational risk; it was not a private diary entry discovered after his death.

Second, it fixes a precise date and approximate time. Many historical UFO reports become difficult to evaluate because witnesses cannot later agree when an event occurred. Lennon's note avoids that uncertainty by recording the observation as occurring on 23 August 1974 at about 9 o'clock.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.johnlennon.com/music/albums/walls-and-bridges/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: johnlennon.com">[John Lennon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">johnlennon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon Walls And Bridges</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John LennonWalls And Bridges - John Lennon with The Plastic Ono Nuclear Band/Little Big Horns/and the Philharmanic OrchestrangeSeptember...</span></span></span>

Third, the inscription corroborates the broad timeline found in later interviews and in May Pang's recollections. Although those later sources provide far more descriptive detail, they are consistent with the basic date and time already preserved in the album artwork.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974...</span></span></span>

For these reasons, the note is often treated as the strongest contemporary documentary evidence that Lennon sincerely reported the experience soon after it happened.

## What the Note Cannot Prove

The evidential limits are just as important as its strengths.

The inscription does **not** establish:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* that the observed object was extraterrestrial;
* that it was impossible to identify by conventional means;
* the object's size, altitude or speed;
* that Lennon correctly interpreted what he observed.

</div>

The wording records only a personal claim. It contains no description of the object's appearance, no corroborating photographs, no measurements and no supporting physical evidence. Even the use of the term "U.F.O." reflects Lennon's own description rather than an independently verified classification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.johnlennon.com/music/albums/walls-and-bridges/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: johnlennon.com">[John Lennon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">johnlennon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon Walls And Bridges</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John LennonWalls And Bridges - John Lennon with The Plastic Ono Nuclear Band/Little Big Horns/and the Philharmanic OrchestrangeSeptember...</span></span></span>

Consequently, the note should not be read as proof of an extraordinary aerial phenomenon. Instead, it is evidence that a well-known witness made a contemporaneous claim which can be historically dated with unusual precision.

## Why Historians Give the Album Note Special Weight

Researchers evaluating eyewitness testimony generally distinguish between evidence for an event and evidence for a person's report of that event. The *Walls and Bridges* inscription falls firmly into the second category.

Its importance comes from several characteristics rarely found together in celebrity UFO cases:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* it was published close to the alleged sighting;
* it identifies a specific date and time;
* it was distributed commercially rather than recalled years later;
* it predates many later interviews and memoirs;
* it can still be examined in surviving editions of the original album artwork.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.johnlennon.com/music/albums/walls-and-bridges/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: johnlennon.com">[John Lennon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">johnlennon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Lennon Walls And Bridges</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John LennonWalls And Bridges - John Lennon with The Plastic Ono Nuclear Band/Little Big Horns/and the Philharmanic OrchestrangeSeptember...</span></span></span>

</div>

Because of these qualities, the note has become the documentary foundation for discussions of John Lennon's 1974 UFO report. Later testimony may add descriptive detail, but the album packaging remains the earliest widely circulated record showing that Lennon himself publicly attached his name to the claim while the experience was still fresh.
