## Introduction

[John Lennon]({{ 'john-lennon/' | relative_url }}) did not wait years to tell people about his reported UFO sighting. Within weeks of the event, he had already alluded to it in the *Walls and Bridges* album artwork, and later in 1974 he publicly described what he claimed to have seen in an interview while promoting the record. That interview is especially important because it preserves Lennon's earliest extended first-person account, recorded before later memoirs, [documentaries]({{ 'documentaries/' | relative_url }}) and UFO literature expanded the story. Rather than presenting a polished legend, it captures Lennon explaining, in his own words, what drew his attention, what the object looked like, and why he concluded it was neither a helicopter nor a balloon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

## What Lennon Said About the UFO in 1974

The most widely cited contemporary description comes from Lennon's November 1974 interview published in *Interview* magazine (then *Andy Warhol's Interview*). During the conversation, Lennon himself steered the discussion towards the small note printed in *Walls and Bridges* stating that he had seen a UFO.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

He described being in his New York apartment on a hot evening, saying he was lying naked on his bed before feeling an urge to look out of the window. As he turned his head, he noticed what he said was an object hovering above a neighbouring building, which he estimated to be about 100 feet away. Although that distance was only his personal impression rather than a measured estimate, it conveyed how unusually close the object appeared to him.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

Several visual details remained remarkably consistent throughout the interview:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* a dark or black central body;
* ordinary-looking white lights flashing around the lower edge;
* a single steady red light on top;
* silent movement despite its apparent proximity.

</div>

One phrase has become especially well known because of its ordinary language. Rather than describing futuristic lights, Lennon said they looked like "ordinary electric light bulbs" blinking on and off around the bottom. This everyday comparison gives the interview an unusually concrete quality and distinguishes it from many more dramatic UFO narratives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

Equally important was what he did **not** report. He made no claim of communication, occupants or extraordinary manoeuvres. His explanation focused almost entirely on the object's appearance and its silence.

## Why He Dismissed Aircraft and Balloons

During the interview Lennon explained his own reasoning rather than immediately calling the object extraterrestrial.

His thought process unfolded in stages. He first wondered whether it was a helicopter but rejected that idea because he could hear the normal sounds of New York traffic while hearing no engine noise from the object itself. He then considered whether it might be a balloon, only to conclude that its controlled movement did not resemble one drifting in the wind.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

This sequence matters historically because it shows Lennon attempting simple conventional explanations before concluding that he could not identify what he had seen. His account therefore fits the literal meaning of an unidentified flying object rather than beginning with a direct assertion of an alien spacecraft.

## His Replies About Drink and Drugs

One of the interviewer's first reactions was to ask whether Lennon had been drunk, high or experiencing effects from primal therapy—a reflection of both his public image during the "Lost Weekend" period and the scepticism surrounding unusual claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

Lennon's response was immediate and unequivocal:

> "No. Actually I was very straight."

He insisted he had not been intoxicated when the sighting occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

That answer has become an important part of later discussions because the sighting occurred during a period in which Lennon was widely known to have struggled with heavy drinking and drug use at other times. Biographical sources make clear that substance misuse formed part of this phase of his life, but that broader reputation does not establish his condition on the specific evening of the reported sighting. The interview preserves his explicit claim that he was sober at the time, even if independent verification is impossible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.biography.com/musicians/john-lennon-lost-weekend" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: biography.com">[Biography]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">biography.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Inside John Lennon&#x27;s &#x27;Lost Weekend&#x27; Period</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Inside John Lennon&#x27;s &#x27;Lost Weekend&#x27; Period...</span></span></span>

## How the Interview Compares with Later Retellings

The 1974 interview serves as a valuable benchmark because it predates later memoirs and popular retellings.

Several core elements remain consistent across subsequent accounts:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* flashing white lights around the rim;
* one steady red light on top;
* dark central body;
* silent movement;
* apparent closeness to the apartment.

</div>

## What Makes the 1974 Interview Historically Significant

The interview occupies an important place in the documentary record of Lennon's UFO story for three reasons.

First, it was contemporary. The account appeared the same year as the reported sighting rather than decades later, reducing concerns that memory alone shaped the narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dangerousminds.net/comments/john_lennon_sees_a_ufo_in_new_york_city_1974/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dangerousminds.net">[Dangerous Minds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dangerousminds.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">john lennon sees a ufo in new york city 1974</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dangerous MindsJohn Lennon sees a UFO in New York City, 1974April 13, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 13, 2015</span></span></span>

Second, it captured Lennon's spontaneous description in conversational form. Instead of offering a carefully constructed retrospective story, he explained what caught his attention, the sequence of his reasoning and the specific visual features that stayed with him.

Third, it established details that remained surprisingly stable in later retellings. Although later accounts added colour and interpretation, the interview already contained the defining characteristics most closely associated with the case: flashing white lights, a single red top light, silent movement and Lennon's insistence that he had been sober when he saw the object.     var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
