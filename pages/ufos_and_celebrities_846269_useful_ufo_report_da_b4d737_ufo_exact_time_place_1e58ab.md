## Introduction

A UFO report becomes scientifically testable only when investigators can determine exactly **when** and **where** the observation occurred. Without a precise timestamp and observing location, it is often impossible to compare a sighting with known aircraft movements, satellite passes, rocket launches, astronomical objects, weather conditions or local viewing geometry. The report may remain interesting, but it cannot easily be verified or ruled out.

This requirement applies equally to high-profile celebrity sightings and reports from unknown witnesses. Public attention does not compensate for missing measurements. NASA's independent study of unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) identified the lack of consistent, high-quality observational data as one of the biggest barriers to meaningful scientific analysis, emphasising that future investigations depend on better documented observations rather than larger numbers of reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/uap/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science UAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA ScienceUAP - NASA ScienceFebruary 23, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 23, 2026</span></span></span>

The goal is not simply to know the date and town. Investigators need enough information to reconstruct the observer's position and the sky as it appeared at that moment.

* **Exact date and local time**, including the time zone and whether the time came from a phone, watch, vehicle clock or later estimate.
* **Start and end times**, or the shortest possible time window if the witness is uncertain.
* **Precise observing location**, preferably GPS coordinates, a street address or a clearly identifiable landmark.

Modern reporting systems reflect these priorities. The National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC), for example, requests an exact date, time, mapped observation point and encourages witnesses to provide latitude and longitude where available because these details allow later comparison with independent datasets.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/reportform-wp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[nuforc.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reportform wp</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sighting Report Form &#124; NUFORCAugust 21, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2021</span></span></span>

Historic government report forms reached similar conclusions. ## How small timing errors change possible matches

Many aerial objects move quickly enough that even modest timing uncertainty can prevent reliable identification.

For example:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* A commercial aircraft travelling at cruising speed moves several kilometres in one minute.
* Low-Earth-orbit satellites cross large sections of the sky within minutes.
* The International Space Station and Starlink satellites can appear unexpectedly bright for only brief periods because of changing illumination.
* Rocket launches produce visible exhaust plumes that evolve rapidly over a few minutes.

</div>

If a witness records "about 9 pm" instead of "20:58:17", investigators may find multiple candidate aircraft, satellite passes or astronomical events, making confident matching much more difficult.

Recent research illustrates this problem clearly. A detailed reconstruction of pilot reports initially considered anomalous showed that matching the exact viewing time, aircraft position and satellite orbital data allowed researchers to identify the objects as a recently launched Starlink satellite train seen under unusual lighting conditions. Without precise timing and location, that reconstruction would not have been possible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08155" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Enhancing Space Situational Awareness to Mitigate Risk: A Single-Case Study in the Misidentification of a Recently-Launched Starlink...</span></span></span>

NASA likewise notes that improving the quality and precision of observational data—including accurate temporal and spatial information—is more valuable than simply collecting larger numbers of reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/uap/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science UAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA ScienceUAP - NASA ScienceFebruary 23, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 23, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why coordinates matter as much as the clock

Location determines what was actually visible from a witness's perspective.

Moving only a few kilometres can alter:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* whether terrain blocked part of the sky;
* whether an aircraft appeared against city lights or open countryside;
* the apparent elevation of planets and bright stars;
* visibility through local cloud gaps;
* the apparent path of satellites near the horizon.

</div>

Exact coordinates also allow investigators to combine multiple independent reports. When witnesses at different locations observe the same object, [triangulation]({{ 'triangulation/' | relative_url }}) may estimate altitude or distance if the viewing directions are accurately known. Without reliable positions, such geometric reconstruction becomes impossible.

This is one reason modern reporting forms increasingly encourage witnesses to mark their exact observation point on an interactive map rather than giving only a nearby town.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/reportform-wp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[nuforc.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reportform wp</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sighting Report Form &#124; NUFORCAugust 21, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2021</span></span></span>

## Reconstructing the observing geometry

Time and place together allow investigators to recreate the sky almost exactly as the witness saw it.

Typical comparisons include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* civil and military aircraft tracks where available;
* satellite orbital predictions;
* rocket launch schedules;
* positions of the Moon, planets and bright stars;
* meteor activity;
* weather observations, cloud cover and visibility;
* sunrise, sunset and twilight conditions.

</div>

Each comparison depends on accurate geometry rather than memory alone.

For example, a bright light reported "above the western horizon shortly after sunset" could correspond to Venus from one location but not another, or it may match the approach path of an aircraft visible only from certain positions. A reconstruction based on precise coordinates and timestamps can distinguish between these possibilities.

## Ways to recover missing timestamps and coordinates

Witnesses often discover only later that they did not note the exact time. Investigators therefore try to recover objective information before relying solely on memory.

Useful methods include:

* **Phone metadata** from photographs or videos, which often records time and GPS location.
* **Message histories**, where a witness texted someone immediately after the sighting.
* **Emergency calls or social media posts**, which establish the latest possible observation time.
* **Vehicle dashcams or home security cameras**, which may contain timestamps.
* **Smartwatch activity logs** showing where the witness was.
* **Maps and navigation history** stored on mobile devices.
* **[Independent witness]({{ 'independent-accounts/' | relative_url }}) accounts**, especially if recorded separately before discussion.

Older investigative guidance has long recommended exploring **time uncertainty** directly. Rather than accepting a single estimate, investigators ask witnesses to identify the earliest and latest possible observation times and to mentally replay the event to narrow the uncertainty window. This approach recognises that unusual events often distort subjective perception of duration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://projectaquarius.mufon.com/wp-content/uploads/APROBulletins/APRO_Field_Investigator_Handbook.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: projectaquarius.mufon.com">[Project Aquarius]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">projectaquarius.mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Aquariushdbk_1.pdf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 8, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 8, 2010</span></span></span>

## Why investigators record uncertainty instead of false precision

Exact-looking numbers are not always accurate.

A witness who reports "20:30" from memory several days later may actually have an uncertainty of plus or minus ten minutes. Recording that uncertainty explicitly is often more scientifically valuable than presenting an unjustified exact time.

Similarly, a location described as "near the motorway" should not be converted into invented GPS coordinates. Instead, investigators document the uncertainty and seek independent evidence that can narrow it.

Professional investigation therefore distinguishes between:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Measured values**, such as GPS coordinates or camera timestamps.
* **Estimated values**, based on witness recollection.
* **Unknown values**, where no reliable reconstruction is possible.

</div>

Maintaining that distinction prevents later analyses from treating uncertain information as established fact.

## What this means for celebrity UFO reports

Celebrity witnesses often generate extensive media coverage, but headlines rarely preserve the details that matter most for verification. Reports may describe an unusual object without specifying the exact observation point, the start time or the duration.

If those basic measurements are absent, investigators cannot reliably compare the account with aviation records, satellite databases or astronomical conditions. Conversely, when a celebrity report includes precise time, location and independently preserved photographs or videos with metadata, it becomes no different from any other potentially testable observation.

From an evidence perspective, the credibility of a UFO report depends far more on reconstructable time and place than on the fame of the person making the claim.

    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
