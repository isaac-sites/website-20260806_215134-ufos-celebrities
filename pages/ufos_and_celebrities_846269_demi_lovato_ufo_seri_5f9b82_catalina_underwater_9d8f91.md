## Introduction

One of the most unusual episodes in *Unidentified with [Demi Lovato]({{ 'demi-lovato/' | relative_url }})* shifted attention from lights in the sky to alleged activity beneath the Pacific Ocean around Santa Catalina Island, off the coast of southern California. The programme explored a long-running belief that unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) and unidentified submerged objects (USOs) may be connected, and that the waters near Catalina could conceal an extraterrestrial underwater base. While the location has accumulated decades of UFO reports, maritime sightings and military stories, the leap from unexplained observations to a hidden alien installation remains unsupported by verifiable evidence. The Catalina case is therefore best understood as an example of how genuine unexplained incidents, military activity and local folklore have combined into one of the most persistent modern UFO legends.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://books.google.com/books/about/Undersea_Ufo_Base.html?id=s2pVtAEACAAJ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: books.google.com">[Google Books]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">books.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Books Undersea UFO Base: An In-Depth Investigation of USOs in the Santa Catalina</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google BooksUndersea UFO Base: An In-Depth Investigation of USOs in the Santa Catalina... - Preston Dennett - Google BooksMarch 7, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 7, 2018</span></span></span>

## Why Catalina attracts UFO claims

Santa Catalina Island occupies an unusual position in southern California. It lies close to heavily used shipping lanes, popular recreational boating routes and extensive US Navy training areas. Aircraft, naval vessels and research platforms routinely operate in the surrounding waters, creating an environment where unusual observations are more likely simply because there is so much activity.

Within UFO culture, Catalina has acquired an even stronger reputation. Researchers have collected reports dating back many decades describing glowing objects over the ocean, lights entering or leaving the water, and unexplained aerial sightings viewed from both the island and the mainland. These accounts have led some enthusiasts to describe the Catalina Channel as one of the world's most active USO locations, although the reports vary greatly in quality and most rely on eyewitness testimony rather than independently verifiable physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://books.google.com/books/about/Undersea_Ufo_Base.html?id=s2pVtAEACAAJ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: books.google.com">[Google Books]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">books.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Books Undersea UFO Base: An In-Depth Investigation of USOs in the Santa Catalina</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google BooksUndersea UFO Base: An In-Depth Investigation of USOs in the Santa Catalina... - Preston Dennett - Google BooksMarch 7, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 7, 2018</span></span></span>

The location also benefits from a feedback effect. As Catalina became known as a UFO hotspot through books, [documentaries]({{ 'documentaries/' | relative_url }}) and television programmes, new sightings were increasingly interpreted through that existing narrative. This does not automatically invalidate eyewitness reports, but it makes it more difficult to separate genuinely unusual events from expectations created by the area's reputation.

## Military sightings and ocean-entry stories

A major reason Catalina features prominently in modern UFO discussions is its proximity to the operating area of the famous 2004 USS *Nimitz* incident.

During those exercises, personnel aboard the USS *Princeton* tracked unusual radar returns, while Commander David Fravor and other aviators reported encountering a smooth, white "Tic Tac"-shaped object displaying flight characteristics they could not readily explain. Fravor also described seeing an area of disturbed water beneath the object before it accelerated away. Because the encounter occurred in the Southern California offshore military training area, later commentators connected it to longstanding Catalina legends.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catalinaufos.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catalinaufos.com">[catalinaufos.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catalinaufos.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on catalinaufos.com.</span></span></span>

Importantly, the publicly available military testimony does **not** state that pilots observed an alien craft entering an underwater base. Instead, later speculation interpreted the disturbed water as evidence that another object might have been submerged below the surface. That interpretation goes well beyond what witnesses themselves reported.

The Catalina episode of *Unidentified with Demi Lovato* drew on this wider body of stories by linking historical local reports with modern military encounters. The programme suggested that recurring observations over both sea and sky could indicate a common source, but it did not present new physical evidence establishing such a connection.

## How the underwater base theory developed

The idea of a hidden base beneath the Catalina Channel emerged gradually rather than from a single incident.

Several recurring themes appear throughout books, documentaries and UFO investigations:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* reports of luminous objects apparently entering or leaving the ocean;
* claims from military or civilian witnesses describing unusual underwater lights;
* speculation that advanced craft could operate seamlessly through air and water ("transmedium" movement);
* suggestions that deep ocean locations would provide concealment from human observation.

</div>

Authors and investigators have argued that these recurring themes collectively point towards an underwater operating base somewhere off southern California. Entire books have been devoted to assembling witness testimony in support of this hypothesis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://books.google.com/books/about/Undersea_Ufo_Base.html?id=s2pVtAEACAAJ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: books.google.com">[Google Books]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">books.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Books Undersea UFO Base: An In-Depth Investigation of USOs in the Santa Catalina</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google BooksUndersea UFO Base: An In-Depth Investigation of USOs in the Santa Catalina... - Preston Dennett - Google BooksMarch 7, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 7, 2018</span></span></span>

In recent years, some commentators have additionally pointed to underwater geological features visible in publicly available bathymetric maps, claiming they resemble artificial structures or entrances. These claims have received considerable attention online but have not been validated by marine geologists or supported by direct physical investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomatrix.org/2026/02/santa-catalina-usos-gallaudet-undersea-base.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomatrix.org">[UFO Matrix &#124; The Disclosure Ages]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomatrix.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Matrix &#124; The Disclosure AgesSanta Catalina USOs: Admiral Gallaudet’s Evidence for an Undersea BaseFebruary 3, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 3, 2026</span></span></span>

## The gap between unidentified objects and alien bases

The Catalina story illustrates an important distinction within UAP research: an unexplained observation is not the same thing as evidence for an extraordinary explanation.

Several facts are reasonably well established:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* numerous UFO reports have originated around southern California over many decades;
* the region hosts intensive military testing and aviation activity;
* some military personnel have described encounters they considered genuinely unexplained;
* the 2004 *Nimitz* encounter remains one of the most studied modern military UAP cases.

</div>

However, none of those facts demonstrate the existence of an extraterrestrial underwater installation.

The underwater-base hypothesis requires additional evidence that has not been produced, such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* confirmed sonar data showing artificial structures;
* recoverable physical materials;
* repeatable instrument observations demonstrating craft entering or leaving a specific location;
* independently verified documentation linking military investigations to an underwater facility.

</div>

Without such evidence, the hypothesis remains speculative rather than established.

## Scientific and geological perspectives

Catalina's underwater landscape is geologically complex. The island sits within an active tectonic region shaped by fault movement, submarine slopes and changing sea levels over long periods.

Research by the US Geological Survey has documented submerged terraces, complicated bathymetry and ongoing geological processes around Catalina. These natural features can appear striking in sonar or bathymetric imagery, particularly when viewed at low resolution or with incomplete datasets. ## Catalina in Demi Lovato's investigation

Within *Unidentified with Demi Lovato*, Catalina served as a bridge between traditional UFO stories and broader ideas about hidden extraterrestrial activity on Earth.

Rather than attempting a systematic marine investigation, the episode brought together eyewitness testimony, historical reports and discussion of military encounters to explore whether unidentified phenomena might originate beneath the ocean as well as in the atmosphere. This reflected a broader trend within contemporary UFO culture, where interest has increasingly expanded from unidentified flying objects to "transmedium" phenomena capable of moving through air and water.

The programme ultimately presented Catalina as an intriguing mystery rather than resolving it. While it highlighted genuine historical sightings and one of the world's best-known military UAP cases, it offered no new evidence demonstrating that an alien base exists beneath the island or the surrounding channel. That distinction remains central to assessing the Catalina claims: the reports of unexplained phenomena are real historical records, but the proposed underwater extraterrestrial installation remains an unverified hypothesis.
