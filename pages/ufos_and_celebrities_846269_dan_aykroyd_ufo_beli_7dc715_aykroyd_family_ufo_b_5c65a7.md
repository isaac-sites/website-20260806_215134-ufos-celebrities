## Introduction

[Dan Aykroyd]({{ 'dan-aykroyd/' | relative_url }})'s interest in UFOs did not emerge in isolation or simply from later celebrity fascination with unexplained phenomena. By his own account, it developed within a family culture where Spiritualism, ghost research, psychic experiences and unusual aerial sightings were treated as legitimate subjects rather than fringe curiosities. That background helps explain why Aykroyd has consistently approached UFO reports as part of a broader paranormal worldview instead of viewing them as an entirely separate mystery. ## Spiritualism was part of the family's identity

The strongest documented influence on Aykroyd's outlook comes from his family's long association with Spiritualism. According to his father, Peter H. Aykroyd, the family's interest stretches back several generations to Dr Samuel Augustus Aykroyd, who held séances, supported mediums and kept extensive journals documenting attempts to investigate psychic phenomena. Rather than presenting these activities as entertainment, Peter Aykroyd described them as sincere efforts to understand whether consciousness survived death.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/594195/a-history-of-ghosts-by-peter-h-aykroyd-with-angela-narth-introduction-by-dan-aykroyd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: penguinrandomhouse.com">[Penguin Random House]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">penguinrandomhouse.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">a history of ghosts by peter h aykroyd with angela narth</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Penguin Random HouseA History of Ghosts by Peter H. The book describes how successive generations of the family grew up around discussions of ghosts, apparitions and psychic research, making such topics an ordinary part of domestic life rather than something exceptional. Dan Aykroyd wrote the introduction and has repeatedly endorsed the book as an accurate account of the environment in which he was raised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/594195/a-history-of-ghosts-by-peter-h-aykroyd-with-angela-narth-introduction-by-dan-aykroyd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: penguinrandomhouse.com">[Penguin Random House]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">penguinrandomhouse.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">a history of ghosts by peter h aykroyd with angela narth</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Penguin Random HouseA History of Ghosts by Peter H. He has described three generations before him as already deeply interested in paranormal investigation, leaving him with little sense that these subjects required special justification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://decider.com/2024/11/15/dan-aykroyd-interview-the-unbelievable-season-2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: decider.com">[Decider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">decider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aykroyd, a spiritualist with a long-standing interest in UFOs, spirits, and different dimensions, narrates true, astonishing stories from...</span></span></span>

## His mother's reported Ottawa sighting

Alongside family interest in ghosts, Aykroyd has frequently cited a story from his mother, Lorraine Aykroyd, as one of the earliest reasons UFOs felt personally significant.

According to Aykroyd, his mother reported seeing an unusual illuminated object over Ottawa in the late 1940s. He has described the incident as involving an object that appeared unlike conventional aircraft and said that the experience remained an accepted family story throughout his childhood. Because it came from someone he trusted, he regarded UFO reports as something that could happen to ordinary people rather than merely sensational newspaper stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2024/11/12/entertainment/dan-aykroyd-on-ufos-and-how-john-belushi-showed-him-the-power-of-stardom/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Known for his comedy work on &quot;Saturday Night Live,&quot; &quot;Ghostbusters,&quot; and &quot;Coneheads,&quot; Aykroyd has a longstanding interest in the paranorma...</span></span></span>

Importantly, the incident exists primarily through later family recollections. No widely available contemporary investigation, official report or physical evidence has emerged that would allow historians or investigators to reconstruct exactly what was observed. As a result, the story is valuable chiefly as evidence of family influence rather than as independent evidence for extraterrestrial visitation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2024/11/12/entertainment/dan-aykroyd-on-ufos-and-how-john-belushi-showed-him-the-power-of-stardom/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Known for his comedy work on &quot;Saturday Night Live,&quot; &quot;Ghostbusters,&quot; and &quot;Coneheads,&quot; Aykroyd has a longstanding interest in the paranorma...</span></span></span>

## Why ghosts and UFOs became connected

Many people treat ghosts, psychic phenomena and UFOs as unrelated subjects. Aykroyd's upbringing encouraged the opposite interpretation.

Within the family's tradition, unexplained experiences were often viewed as different manifestations of a wider reality that conventional science had not yet fully explained. Instead of placing ghosts, psychic experiences and unidentified aerial objects into separate categories, they were understood as potentially connected expressions of unknown forces or dimensions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://decider.com/2024/11/15/dan-aykroyd-interview-the-unbelievable-season-2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: decider.com">[Decider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">decider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aykroyd, a spiritualist with a long-standing interest in UFOs, spirits, and different dimensions, narrates true, astonishing stories from...</span></span></span>
