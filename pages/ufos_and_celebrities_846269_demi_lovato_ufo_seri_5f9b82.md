## How the four-part search was structured

Peacock presented the series as an unscripted investigation rather than a conventional documentary. Lovato, who also served as an executive producer, travelled with Dallas and Montgomery, consulting UFO enthusiasts, therapists, eyewitnesses and paranormal investigators. The original commissioning description promised recent encounter reports, government material, expert consultations and tests at alleged hotspots. It also made Lovato’s intended conclusion unusually clear: the series would try to persuade friends, family and followers that intelligent extraterrestrial beings existed and were already present on Earth.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.c21media.net/news/lovato-ufo-series-joins-peacock-unscripted/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: c21media.net">[C21media]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">c21media.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lovato UFO series joins Peacock unscripted &#124; News &#124; C21Media</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lovato UFO series joins Peacock unscripted &#124; News &#124; C21MediaMay 12, 2021 — Lovato UFO series joins Peacock unscripted &#124; News &#124; C2...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2021</span></span></span>

## Personal experience drove the investigation

Lovato’s interest pre-dated the series. In 2020, the singer visited Joshua Tree in California with Steven Greer, a prominent advocate of meditation-based extraterrestrial contact, and subsequently posted that the trip had produced profound sightings. Lovato also discussed UFOs, astral projection and contact meditation on Kesha’s podcast before Peacock announced the programme.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.iheart.com/content/2021-01-08-demi-lovato-talks-aliens-astral-projection-on-kesha-and-the-creepies/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iheart.com">[iHeart]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iheart.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on iheart.com.</span></span></span>

During publicity for *Unidentified*, Lovato described seeing a [blue orb]({{ 'blue-orb/' | relative_url }}) in the Joshua Tree desert, reportedly about 50 feet away and hovering roughly 10 or 15 feet above the ground. The experience was characterised as beautiful rather than threatening and became the series’ motivational story: Lovato wanted to understand what had happened and show that extraterrestrial beings should not necessarily be feared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/music/news/demi-lovato-ufo-encounter-b1928232.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: independent.co.uk">[independent.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">independent.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on independent.co.uk.</span></span></span>

This personal investment gave the programme an emotional coherence that many UFO productions lack. Lovato was not merely presenting other people’s cases but searching for confirmation of an experience already interpreted as meaningful. The series also connected that search to questions of survival, healing and protection following Lovato’s widely discussed near-death experience. In the hypnosis sequence, the beings Lovato described functioned less like observable visitors than protective figures within a personal spiritual narrative. Vanity Fair’s account of the episode noted descriptions of small humanoids, a brightly coloured planet and “light doctors” who were supposedly protecting Lovato from self-harm.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2021/10/all-the-questions-i-had-while-watching-unidentified-with-demi-lovato" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vanityfair.com">[Vanity Fair]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vanityfair.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">all the questions i had while watching unidentified with demi lovato</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">In the premiere episode of their new Peacock series Unidentified with Demi Lovato, the pop star unpacks their fascination with the great...</span></span></span>

That does not make the experience insincere. It does, however, change the evidential question. A psychologically or spiritually meaningful episode may be important to the person experiencing it without establishing that an external spacecraft, creature or communication occurred. The series rarely maintained that distinction for long.

## Hotspots, witnesses and attempted contact

The programme’s road-trip structure allowed each location to provide a different kind of mystery. The first episode moved from Lovato’s dreamlike or out-of-body recollection to people who identified themselves as abductees. A support-group setting gave participants space to describe frightening or extraordinary experiences, while a therapist used hypnosis to explore Lovato’s own memories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2021/10/all-the-questions-i-had-while-watching-unidentified-with-demi-lovato" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vanityfair.com">[Vanity Fair]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vanityfair.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">all the questions i had while watching unidentified with demi lovato</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">In the premiere episode of their new Peacock series Unidentified with Demi Lovato, the pop star unpacks their fascination with the great...</span></span></span>

As human testimony, these scenes offered an informative glimpse into the social world of alleged experiencers: people exchanged narratives, recognised patterns in one another’s stories and treated unusual memories as evidence of contact. As an investigation, however, the process risked reinforcing a shared interpretation before alternative explanations had been examined. No physical trace or independently verified record emerged from the hypnosis session.

The second episode expanded the premise from UFOs into ghost hunting. At Vulture City, Lovato and paranormal investigators used devices intended to register or translate the presence of unseen entities. A widely circulated scene showed Lovato singing part of “Skyscraper” after equipment was interpreted as indicating that an entity called Carmen disliked the men in the room. Lovato later said the scene was embarrassing but funny in retrospect and acknowledged being heavily under the influence of cannabis while filming it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://people.com/demi-lovato-was-so-stoned-sang-to-traumatized-ghost-11809242" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: people.com">[People.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">people.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on people.com.</span></span></span>

The sequence illustrates the series’ entertainment logic. Ambiguous sounds or device responses were given personalities, motives and emotional reactions, creating an immediate story for viewers. But the programme did not demonstrate that the equipment reliably detected either ghosts or extraterrestrials, much less that it could distinguish one from the other.

Around Santa Catalina Island, the investigation moved closer to recognisable UFO subject matter. The episode considered maritime sightings and the idea that apparently extraordinary craft might enter the ocean or operate from underwater. Catalina has become prominent in UFO [storytelling]({{ 'storytelling/' | relative_url }}) because it lies near military training areas and has accumulated decades of sighting claims. Yet the programme’s leap from unidentified reports to hidden extraterrestrial bases remained speculative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/unidentified-demi-lovato-peacock-ufo-show-4-things-we-learned" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: syfy.com">[SYFY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">syfy.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unidentified with Demi Lovato: 4 things we learned from the Peacock series</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unidentified with Demi Lovato: 4 things we learned from the Peacock series</span></span></span>

The Sedona finale then used conscious-contact practices: participants meditated, directed intentions towards the sky and watched for lights or responses. This approach can create a powerful group experience, particularly in a dramatic landscape under dark skies. It cannot by itself establish causation. A light appearing after a request may be an aircraft, satellite, meteor or other ordinary object; interpreting its timing as a reply depends on the participants’ expectations rather than a controlled test.

## Why the methods did not prove contact

The largest evidential problem was the absence of a clear procedure for distinguishing an extraordinary event from a mistaken identification, a psychological experience or a production effect. The series collected impressions, reactions and suggestive coincidences, but it did not consistently provide calibrated measurements, complete metadata, comparison trials or independent replication.

**Regressive hypnosis was especially weak as a truth-finding tool.** Hypnosis may help someone relax or construct a coherent account, but vividness and confidence do not guarantee historical accuracy. Research reviews warn that hypnotic regression and guided imagery can unintentionally encourage false memories, particularly when the session assumes that a hidden event exists and needs to be recovered.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1433762/full" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: frontiersin.org">[frontiersin.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">frontiersin.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on frontiersin.org.</span></span></span>

The issue is not that every hypnotically recalled image must be false. It is that the method cannot reliably separate authentic memory from imagination, suggestion or later reconstruction. Lovato’s hypnosis therefore supplied material for understanding personal belief, not independent confirmation of an abduction.

**The paranormal devices created a similar interpretation problem.** When a detector produced a noise, word or changing reading, the programme often treated the result as responsive communication. A stronger test would have required information about the device’s normal error rate, environmental interference, operator influence and whether comparable responses occurred during control sessions. Without such safeguards, scattered outputs can be assembled into apparent dialogue after the fact.

**The skywatching sessions lacked sufficient observational data.** NASA’s independent UAP study has emphasised that unusual sightings are difficult to resolve when sensors were not designed or calibrated for the task, or when recordings lack time, location, environmental and instrument metadata. It recommends systematic observations, clear [evidence thresholds]({{ 'evidence-threshold/' | relative_url }}) and multiple forms of corroborating sensor data.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report-0.pdf?emrc=6a56289587df4" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasa.gov">[NASA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uap independent study team final report 0</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">uap independent study team final report 0</span></span></span>

A television crew could, in principle, have deployed synchronised cameras, recorded precise bearings, checked aircraft and satellite traffic, logged weather conditions and published unedited files for outside review. *Unidentified* instead prioritised the emotional moment when a participant believed contact had occurred. That choice was understandable for entertainment, but it limited the programme’s scientific value.

## Entertainment value versus evidential value

As celebrity television, the series had several strengths. Lovato brought a large audience to a subject often presented through military language or specialised UFO personalities. The participation of a close friend and sibling created an approachable dynamic, and Montgomery’s doubts gave viewers permission to remain unconvinced. The programme also treated alleged experiencers with sympathy rather than ridicule, potentially reducing the shame that can surround reports of strange perceptions or frightening sleep experiences.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.c21media.net/news/lovato-ufo-series-joins-peacock-unscripted/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: c21media.net">[C21media]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">c21media.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lovato UFO series joins Peacock unscripted &#124; News &#124; C21Media</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lovato UFO series joins Peacock unscripted &#124; News &#124; C21MediaMay 12, 2021 — Lovato UFO series joins Peacock unscripted &#124; News &#124; C2...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2021</span></span></span>

Its openness also made visible how modern UFO culture can blend several different traditions: unexplained aerial sightings, alien-abduction narratives, mediumship, ghost hunting, New Age spirituality and theories about hidden government knowledge. The series did not invent that mixture, but its celebrity format packaged it for a mainstream streaming audience.

The risk was that respectful listening repeatedly became factual endorsement. A witness’s sincerity, a hypnotic image, a device-generated word and a moving light were all capable of being folded into the same extraterrestrial storyline. Alternative explanations usually received less screen time because they offered less dramatic payoff. Even the nominal scepticism functioned mainly as a character relationship: friends reacted, hesitated and were gradually impressed, rather than conducting sustained tests capable of disproving the programme’s preferred hypothesis.

The series therefore works best as a portrait of celebrity belief and the production of UFO entertainment. It shows how a personal sighting can become the organising story for a television project; how evocative locations and specialist guests can deepen that story; and how editing can turn ambiguous experiences into a journey towards confirmation. It does not establish that Lovato encountered extraterrestrial beings, that ghosts and UFOs share an origin, or that meditation summoned intelligent visitors.

## What the series ultimately investigated

Despite its title, *Unidentified with Demi Lovato* investigated more than unidentified objects. Its real subject was Lovato’s effort to give meaning to unusual experiences and to test a hopeful belief that non-human beings might be benevolent, spiritually significant and accessible through consciousness.

The programme produced memorable encounters and a revealing tour of contemporary UFO subculture. Its locations, witnesses and contact rituals gave viewers a strong sense of participation. What it did not produce was a documented case that outside investigators could independently analyse and reproduce. The four episodes are consequently more valuable as evidence of how celebrity, personal conviction and reality-television storytelling shape public ideas about UFOs than as evidence that extraterrestrial contact occurred.

    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
